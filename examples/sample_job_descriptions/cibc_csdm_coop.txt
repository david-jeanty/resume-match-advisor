What You'll Be Doing
As a Co-op Student on the CSDM (Client Static Data Management) Business Transformation team, you will support data quality and remediation initiatives for Capital Markets products. You will work alongside business analysts and technology partners to identify data issues, coordinate remediation efforts, and support regulatory reporting requirements.

Responsibilities:
- Support data quality and remediation activities across multiple Capital Markets products
- Conduct data investigation to identify root causes of data issues and anomalies
- Prepare structured reporting summaries and KPI trend analysis for management
- Support business analysis activities including requirements gathering and process documentation
- Assist with User Acceptance Testing (UAT) and system testing for CSDM technology initiatives
- Coordinate follow-up with internal stakeholders across multiple workstreams
- Support regulatory reporting deliverables and governance documentation
- Participate in Agile/Scrum ceremonies with the technology delivery team
- Identify and escalate process gaps and control weaknesses

How You'll Succeed
- Business analysis: Apply structured problem solving to data quality and process issues, working with stakeholders to document requirements and drive remediation.
- Data investigation: Use tools like Excel to investigate data anomalies, flag exceptions, and support root-cause analysis.
- Communication: Work with internal clients and technology partners to coordinate solution discussions and status updates.
