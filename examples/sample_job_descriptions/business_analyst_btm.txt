Business Analyst Intern (BTM Co-op) — Winter 2027
Northline Financial Group, Toronto, ON

- Prepare status reports and project documentation in Confluence
- Analyze data in Excel and SQL to support decision making

Required qualifications
- Currently enrolled in a Business Technology Management, MIS, or related business program
- Strong stakeholder communication and documentation skills
- Experience with Excel, including pivot tables and lookups
- Understanding of business analysis or process mapping concepts
- Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment

Preferred qualifications
- Exposure to Jira, Confluence, or Visio
- Basic SQL knowledge
- Experience with agile or scrum from coursework or projects
- Case competition or student consulting experience is an asset
